About Us

Modern Living, Redefined

Musandile Apartments offers a perfect blend of comfort, security, and convenience for families and professionals.

Your New Home in Lusaka

Located on Plot 9712 8th St, Musandile Apartments is a newly built complex featuring spacious two-bedroom apartments, each with a master self-contained layout. Every unit is designed with attention to detail, ensuring a pleasant living experience.

Residents enjoy modern amenities including tiled interiors, a borehole for consistent water supply, and comprehensive security services. Garbage collection is also included, ensuring a clean and comfortable living environment.
